So Far

Sept 2024, Present · Austin, TX

Business Analyst Associate

Teacher Retirement System of Texas

At TRS, the work has been about closing the gap between raw data and the decisions that depend on it. One of the more meaningful things I worked on was building a Databricks pipeline that connected survey data directly into Power BI, a process that had previously required manual exports and hand-offs that delayed leadership by days. Beyond the pipeline work, I have also been focused on redesigning how workflows move through the organization, bringing cycle times down by 35% on event coordination processes that had grown unnecessarily complicated over time.

May 2024, Aug 2024 · Orlando, FL

Business Development Reporting Analyst Intern

Orlando Health

The internship at Orlando Health was where I learned what it actually means to do healthcare analytics. I spent the summer digging into Epic EHR referral and claims data, mapping where patient access was slowing and why. The analysis pointed to real bottlenecks in the authorization process, and the workflow changes that followed contributed to a 30% increase in patient volume and 200 additional authorizations processed each quarter. What stuck with me most was how much the upfront conversations with clinical staff shaped what I even thought to look for in the data.

Jan 2023, Aug 2023 · Remote

Release of Information Specialist

Verisma

Working in healthcare compliance gave me a different kind of data fluency, one built around precision and accountability rather than dashboards. Every month I was handling 500+ medical record requests, each one requiring the right information to reach the right party without anything going wrong. It was detail-oriented, high-stakes work, and it gave me a strong foundation in how healthcare data actually moves through a system, what HIPAA requires in practice, and how to catch process problems before they compound.

June 2022, Feb 2024 · Orlando, FL

Member Information Specialist

Exos

Exos was where I first built something in Power BI that people actually used to make decisions. I was tracking population health KPIs across member programs and noticed patterns in the engagement data that pointed to churn before it happened. The dashboards I built with DAX and Power Query gave the business real-time visibility into program performance, and the findings fed directly into an incentive redesign that improved program efficacy by 25% and brought member participation up by 15%.
