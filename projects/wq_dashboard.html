Key Finding

Replacing the manual DAR scheduling system with Work Queue (WQ) enforcement improved insurance payment compliance by +1.3 percentage points after the March 1 go-live, generating an estimated 251 additional paid claims per month and $63,865 in additional monthly revenue ($766K annualized). Volume grew 58% in the After period; scheduling accuracy and lead time held steady, confirming no operational trade-off from the new system.

Recommendation

WQ enforcement demonstrably improves scheduling compliance and insurance payment rates with no operational cost. Expand WQ rollout to all Orlando Health imaging service lines. Priority targets: South Orange (15,561 cases at 69.1% payment rate, highest volume, most revenue upside), Altamonte Springs (64.6% accuracy, lowest in network, highest compliance risk), and Bayfront St. Petersburg (43.7% payment rate, distinct payor mix requiring separate investigation). Full-network improvement of 1.3pp at current volume represents an estimated $766K+ in incremental annual revenue.
